\# GAP ANALYSIS



\## Existing Process vs Proposed Odoo Solution



| Business Area | AS-IS Situation | Proposed TO-BE Solution |

|---|---|---|

| Inventory tracking | Manual records | Centralized Odoo stock records |

| Stock monitoring | Periodic checks | System-based quantity visibility |

| Replenishment | Reactive purchasing | Configured reordering rules |

| Procurement | Manual coordination | Integrated RFQ and PO workflow |

| Goods receipt | Manual updates | Validated warehouse receipts |

| Order tracking | Disconnected records | Centralized purchase records |



\## Functional Gap Assessment



| ID | Identified Gap | Odoo Functionality |

|---|---|---|

| GAP-01 | Inconsistent stock records | Inventory Management |

| GAP-02 | Late shortage identification | Reordering Rules |

| GAP-03 | Unstructured purchasing | Purchase RFQs |

| GAP-04 | Limited order tracking | Purchase Orders |

| GAP-05 | Delayed inventory updates | Goods Receipt Validation |



\## Conclusion



Standard Odoo Inventory and Purchase functionality addresses the identified process gaps through centralized stock management, replenishment proposals, purchasing workflows and receipt validation.



The case study demonstrates these improvements through a controlled sample transaction.
