\# INVENTORY \& PROCUREMENT PROCESS IMPROVEMENT

\## Odoo ERP Functional Analysis Case Study



\### 1. Executive Summary



This project evaluates how Odoo ERP can improve inventory visibility and procurement coordination for a hypothetical automotive component manufacturer.



The implementation demonstrates integrated Inventory and Purchase workflows using Odoo Community Edition.



\### 2. Business Challenge



The assumed existing business process relied on manual stock tracking and reactive procurement, creating risks of inventory discrepancies and raw material shortages.



\### 3. Implemented Solution



\- Configured raw material product records.

\- Created supplier records.

\- Configured inventory reordering rules.

\- Generated replenishment proposals.

\- Created and confirmed purchase orders.

\- Validated warehouse goods receipts.

\- Verified inventory quantity updates.



\### 4. Demonstrated Results



The Steel Sheets scenario demonstrated the following:



| Metric | Result |

|---|---:|

| Initial stock | 100 units |

| Minimum stock | 20 units |

| Maximum stock | 100 units |

| Simulated consumption | 85 units |

| Remaining stock | 15 units |

| Replenishment proposal | 85 units |

| Final stock | 100 units |



\### 5. Business Value



The configured workflow demonstrates improved stock visibility, structured replenishment, procurement traceability and inventory updates following goods receipt.



No quantified operational savings or real-world production improvements were measured.



\### 6. Project Limitations



\- Hypothetical company and business scenario.

\- Sample product and supplier records.

\- One raw material completed through the full replenishment cycle.

\- No manufacturing or accounting implementation.

\- No custom code or external system integrations.



\### 7. Tools and Skills



Odoo Community Edition, Inventory Management, Purchase Management, Business Requirement Analysis, Process Mapping, Gap Analysis, Functional Configuration and UAT.



\### 8. Conclusion



The case study demonstrates how standard Odoo Inventory and Purchase functionality can support a structured raw material replenishment and procurement process.
