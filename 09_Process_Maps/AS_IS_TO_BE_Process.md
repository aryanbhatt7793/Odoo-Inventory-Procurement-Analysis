\# BUSINESS PROCESS ANALYSIS



\## 1. AS-IS Process – Existing Workflow



1\. Warehouse staff maintain raw material quantities manually.

2\. Stock records are updated periodically.

3\. Procurement identifies shortages after stock levels become insufficient.

4\. Purchase requests are communicated manually.

5\. Purchase orders are created and tracked separately.

6\. Received materials are manually recorded in inventory records.



\### Identified Bottlenecks

\- Delayed stock visibility

\- Inventory discrepancies

\- Reactive procurement

\- Limited purchase order tracking

\- Risk of production material shortages



\## 2. TO-BE Process – Proposed Odoo Workflow



1\. Maintain raw material records in Odoo Inventory.

2\. Configure minimum and maximum stock levels.

3\. Monitor stock availability.

4\. Identify replenishment requirements.

5\. Generate RFQs through Odoo Purchase.

6\. Confirm purchase orders.

7\. Receive materials through warehouse receipts.

8\. Validate receipts to update inventory quantities.



\## 3. Demonstrated Business Scenario



Product: Steel Sheets



\- Initial inventory: 100 units

\- Minimum stock: 20 units

\- Maximum stock: 100 units

\- Simulated consumption: 85 units

\- Remaining stock: 15 units

\- Replenishment proposal: 85 units

\- Confirmed purchase quantity: 85 units

\- Final inventory: 100 units



\## 4. Process Outcome



The demonstrated workflow connects inventory replenishment with procurement and goods receipt, providing a structured method for managing raw material availability.
